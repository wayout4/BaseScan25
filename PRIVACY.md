# Privacy Notice — BaseScan25
Last updated: 13 October 2026

This is a working draft. Review and adapt it to actual deployment, vendors and applicable law before relying on it as a final legal notice.

## Information submitted
When you request a preview, the selected network and asset identifier are sent to the service backend to request public research data. When you start checkout, the service may receive your email address, order identifier, Stripe Checkout session identifier, payment status and report data needed to fulfill the order.

Payment details are entered on Stripe-hosted Checkout. BaseScan25 should not receive or store full card numbers or security codes. Never submit a private key or recovery phrase.

## Use and providers
Information is used to provide previews, associate orders with reports, process payment status, prevent duplicate fulfillment, support customers and secure the service. Third-party public-data providers may receive the asset identifier and network. Stripe processes checkout and payment information under its own terms and privacy notice.

## Retention and deletion
Retain order and report data only as long as needed for delivery, support, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations. The operator must configure and document a real retention period and deletion-request process before launch; this draft does not claim that a deletion workflow is already implemented.

## Security
Keep secrets in server-side environment configuration, never public HTML or source files. The website should never ask for wallet recovery phrases or private keys. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

## Contact
For support or privacy requests, the current route is https://github.com/wayout4/BaseScan25/issues. Replace this with a monitored business contact channel before broader promotion.

This notice may change as data practices change. It is not legal advice.
