Due-diligence checklist

1. Match the address

Check the full contract address against a trustworthy project source.

2. Inspect source

Look for verified source, owner privileges, minting, blacklist and pause controls.

3. Check liquidity

Confirm pool depth, trading history, holders and whether liquidity can be removed.

4. Test cautiously

Avoid unsolicited claim links, unlimited approvals and trades you cannot afford to lose.
